Saved Places

Bring the places you saved in Google Maps here, see them all on one map and in one numbered list, put them in your own order, remove the ones you don't need and add new ones.

How to get your saved places out of Google Maps
  1. Open Google Takeout and sign in with the account you use in Google Maps.
  2. Press Deselect all, then tick only Saved (your lists: Favourites, Want to go, Starred places and any list you made) and Maps (your places) (starred places with their exact location, plus Home, Work and other labeled places).
  3. Press Next step, keep Export once and .zip, and press Create export.
  4. Google emails you a download link, usually within minutes, sometimes a few hours. Download the .zip.
  5. Drop the whole .zip below (no need to unzip it), or pick the .csv and .json files inside it.

Google's list files (.csv) don't include coordinates, only names and links. Places that are also in your starred places get their exact spot; for the rest, press Find them on the map and the page looks each one up by name. Those are marked “approx.”, so check them and use Move pin if one landed in the wrong spot.

Have a map in Google My Maps? Open it, use the ⋮ menu, Export to KML/KMZ, and drop that file here too. Changes you make on this page stay on dwine1.xyz; they don't change your Google account. To bring places back into Google, use Export → KML and import it into a new My Maps map.
